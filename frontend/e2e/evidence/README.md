# E2E evidence

Point-in-time captures produced by `e2e/evidence.spec.ts` and `e2e/submit-flow.spec.ts`
against Arc Testnet. They are a record of one verification run, not living documentation.

`submit-flow.txt` / `submit-flow.png` were captured on 2026-09-16, when the admin panel
was still in Chinese. They have not been retranslated — rewriting a captured snapshot
would misrepresent what the UI actually showed at that moment. They will be replaced
with English captures the next time `submit-flow.spec.ts` runs against a market that is
LOCKED and still inside its settlement window.

The remaining files were re-captured on 2026-09-17 after the UI was translated.
